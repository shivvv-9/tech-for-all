⚠️ Stay Safe from Online Scams

Learn to recognise and avoid common online frauds.

🔒
Golden Rule:

Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, bank password or confidential information with anyone.

🔐 OTP Fraud

Someone may call pretending to be a bank employee and ask for your OTP.

✅ DO: End the call and contact your bank yourself.
❌ DON'T: Share your OTP with anyone.

🏦 Fake KYC Calls

Fraudsters may say your bank account will be blocked unless you provide information immediately.

✅ DO: Verify through your official bank app.
❌ DON'T: Download unknown applications.

🎁 Lottery Scams

Messages claiming that you won a lottery are commonly used to steal money.

✅ DO: Ignore and report the message.
❌ DON'T: Pay money to claim a prize.

📱 WhatsApp Family Scam

Someone may pretend to be your child or relative and urgently ask for money.

✅ DO: Call the person on their known number.
❌ DON'T: Send money immediately.

💳 UPI Scam

Remember: you don't need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.

✅ DO: Verify the receiver's name.
❌ DON'T: Enter your PIN to receive money.

🖥️ Screen Sharing Scam

Fraudsters may ask you to install an app that lets them control or view your phone.

✅ DO: Refuse unknown screen-sharing requests.
❌ DON'T: Give strangers remote access.

🚨 Remember These Rules

  • Never share OTP.
  • Never share your UPI PIN.
  • Never share your ATM PIN.
  • Never install unknown apps.
  • Never click suspicious links.
  • Always verify before sending money.
  • When in doubt, ask a trusted family member.

🔴 What to do if you get scammed

1️⃣ Stay calm and don't make further payments.

2️⃣ Call Cyber Crime Helpline 1930.

3️⃣ Contact your bank and block your card if necessary.

4️⃣ Take screenshots of suspicious messages.

5️⃣ Tell a trusted family member.

6️⃣ Report the incident to the appropriate authorities.
